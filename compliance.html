Compliance Centre

Compliance is a workflow, not a checkbox.

Political and electoral activity can involve election law, government rules, privacy, intellectual property, accessibility, advertising, data protection and jurisdiction-specific notifications.

Election compliance

Check the applicable Election Commission / State Election Commission rules, notifications, candidate and campaign requirements, expenditure rules and local directions for the relevant election.

Legal source registry

Use the official legal text and current notifications as the primary record. Maintain source date, jurisdiction and version.

Privacy & data

Collect only what is necessary, communicate purpose, protect access, define retention and provide appropriate user controls.

AI governance

Document model use, source retrieval, permissions, human review, logging, uncertainty and escalation for high-impact outputs.

Accessibility

Design toward WCAG principles: perceivable, operable, understandable and robust experiences.

Brand & intellectual property

Use original NPSN assets and complete formal name/trademark clearance before commercial or institutional rollout.


Not legal advice: This centre is an operational framework. Applicable law depends on the activity, jurisdiction and date. Obtain qualified legal advice where required.
